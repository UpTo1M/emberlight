How to play

You are the last ember in a dark world. Your flame drains the whole time, so every route is a choice.

Move — WASD / arrow keys, or hold the mouse button to drift toward the cursor.
Light braziers — stand close to a cold brazier until it catches. Every lit brazier feeds your flame and scores points. Lighting braziers quickly in a row builds a combo.
Fireflies — catch them for flame and points.
Shadow wisps — they hunt your light. A touch steals a big chunk of flame and leaves you flickering.
Win a level by lighting every brazier. Lose when your flame burns out. Clear all 8 levels to win.
